Bal Krishna Kharal

I study how heat pumps and HVAC systems perform in real buildings. My research combines building energy simulation, measured data, and EnergyPlus and Modelica co-simulation to compare air-source, ground-source, and multi-source heat pump systems, with a focus on cold climates.

Before my Ph.D., I worked as an HVAC engineer at Thermotech Pvt. Ltd. in Kathmandu, Nepal, designing and commissioning systems for residential, commercial, and industrial buildings. I am open to energy modeling and HVAC roles.

Research

My research uses building energy simulation and measured data to understand how HVAC systems perform in existing buildings, with a focus on heat pumps in cold climates.

Calibrated building energy models

I build whole-building energy models and calibrate them against measured data, following ASHRAE Guideline 14.

Multi-source heat pumps

I compare air-source, ground-source, and multi-source heat pump systems across different climates.

EnergyPlus and Modelica co-simulation

I use Spawn of EnergyPlus to connect detailed building models with dynamic HVAC and control models.

AI-assisted calibration of a residential energy model

Ph.D. research, 2026, journal paper submitted

An automated workflow that calibrates an EnergyPlus model of an occupied house against field measurements. It combines Morris sensitivity screening, genetic algorithm optimization, and an AI agent that runs and checks the simulations.

  • EnergyPlus
  • Python
  • Sensitivity analysis
  • Genetic algorithm
  • AI agents

Model calibration and heat pump evaluation in an occupied house

M.S. thesis, 2026

Calibrated an EnergyPlus model of a residential building using field measurements, following ASHRAE Guideline 14, then used it to compare heat pump options, including a multi-source heat pump.

  • EnergyPlus
  • OpenStudio
  • ASHRAE Guideline 14
  • Field data
